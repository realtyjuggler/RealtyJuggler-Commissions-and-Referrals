# Chapter 3: Referrals and Lead Generation

## Referrals and Lead Generation

Building a pipeline of deals takes time and patience. Leads need to be generated and cultivated, and some may not bear fruit for months or even years. The starting point of every agent's pipeline is leads from marketing activities, referrals from past clients, and networking. RealtyJuggler lets you track these activities and determine what is working and what is not. You can track:

- Leads generated from your marketing activities
- Referrals you have received
- Referrals you have provided to other realtors

This lets you focus your energy on what works and abandon or modify approaches that fail to produce results.

## Tracking the Source of Your Leads / Referrals

The first step in tracking leads is to fill in the Referral Source and Source Info fields for each of your Prospect records. The Prospect Source field should contain the general source of the lead. The Source Info field can hold specific information, such as the name of the referral.

Buyer, Listing, Closing and Rental records also have Source and Source Info fields, but they are located in the Main:Closing:Parties:Party section of the record.

Contact records have a "Referred By:" field, which combines the Referral Source and Source Info fields.

> Image: Screenshot of a "Referred By:" field with a blue drop-down arrow, containing the text "Website", with a blue "Map" link to its right.

Edit the list of lead sources at Main:Settings:Preferences:General. Scroll down to the Lead Sources text field and enter one lead source per line. If you have an uncommon lead source, you do not need to add it to the master list. Type it into the Referred By text field instead of picking from the yellow drop-down.

## Referral Map

Once you have entered the Referred By / Referral Source for several Prospects, Buyers, Listings, Closings or Contacts, you can review visually what is working by clicking the Map link to the right of the Referred By / Referral Source fields. The Referral Map shows a pie chart and tree view of your referral sources.

## Getting Leads from Agents and Other People

Referrals from other agents and friends have always had a special place for experienced realtors. These leads need special attention because they require cultivation and, in some cases, financial compensation.

To track them, go into your deal record (Prospect, Buyer, Listing or Closing) and click the Parties tab. Look for a party with a Role of Referral. If none exists, create one by clicking the "New Party" button at the top of the screen.

> Image: Screenshot of a First Name field containing "Larry", with a yellow drop-down menu open showing "LookUp Contact", "Add to Contacts" and "Help".

If the person who gave you the referral is already in your Contacts database, click the "First Name" label and choose "LookUp Contact" from the yellow drop-down menu, then find and link the contact. To add the person to your Contacts database, fill in their contact information and select "Add To Contacts". It is to your advantage to have every person who has provided you a lead in your Contacts database.

Next, select "Referral" for the role.

If you received a referral from another agent, check the "Agent" check box. Additional fields appear that let you track the direction of the referral and any referral fee you agreed to. Entering this information automatically updates your commission calculation to reflect any referral payment due.

> Image: Screenshot of the referral party fields. Role is "Referral". The Agent checkbox is checked. Direction is "Gave Client Referral (Other Agent Pays You)". Below are "Referral Fee %" with an empty box, Contract set to "None", Last Contact "No Date", and "Contact again in:" with an empty days box.

## Providing Referrals to Other Agents

Providing referrals to other agents can be financially rewarding. RealtyJuggler helps you watch the deal to make sure the referral results in a happy client, and lets you track expected referral fees to make sure they are paid on time. To set up a referral to another agent:

1. Create a new Buyer or Listing record and set its status to "GaveReferral".
2. Click the Commission tab and enter commission information for the deal.
3. Click the Parties tab. Look for a party with a Role of Buyer Agent (or Listing Agent) that represents the agent you gave the referral to. If none exists, create one with the "New Party" button at the top of the screen. If the agent is already in your Contacts database, click the "First Name" label, choose "LookUp Contact" from the yellow drop-down menu, and link the contact. To add the agent to your Contacts database, fill in the contact information and select "Add To Contacts".
4. Enter a value in the "Contact every __ days" field so you are reminded to contact the agent periodically.
5. Eventually, transfer the Buyer or Listing record to Closing using the Transfer tab on the left. In each case, the Income section of RealtyJuggler reflects the income from your referral fee.

> Image: Screenshot of the commission calculator with "Your Side" and "Other Side" tabs. Sale Price $150000; Calculate Commission "Before Concession"; Sales Commission % 3 (Your Side); Your Gross Commission $4500.00; Referral "Received Client Referral (You Pay Other Agent)" with Referral Fee % and Referral $0.00; "Agent & Broker Split Franchise Fee of:" 6% giving Franchise Fee Amount $270.00; Your Split % 100 giving Your Split $4230.00; and Your Net Commission $4230.00.

By following these steps you can track the referral you gave in as much detail as you wish. Enter closing dates, sales price and additional parties as that information becomes available. Finally, calculate your referral fee amount and it is displayed in the Income section of RealtyJuggler. More information is in Video Tutorial 48.

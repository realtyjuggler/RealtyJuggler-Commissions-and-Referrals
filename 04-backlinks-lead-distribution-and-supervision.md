# Chapter 4: BackLinks, Lead Distribution and Supervision

## Parties and BackLinks

When you link a Referral, Buyer Agent or Listing Agent party to your contacts list by clicking the "First Name:" label and selecting "LookUp Contacts" from the yellow menu, you save yourself the trouble of looking up their contact information. You also get an easy way to track all the deals that person has been involved in.

For example, suppose you have worked with a particular loan office on many deals. In some, the loan officer was a referral, and in others the "Loan Officer" party in your own deals. To see all the deals the loan officer has been involved with, open the loan officer's contact record and click the "BackLinks" tab on the left. You see a summary of every deal that loan officer has been part of, and you can click any summary line to go to that record.

Tracking the relationships between Contacts and deals helps you understand which contacts provide the most value and which relationships to deepen. Together with the referral map at Main:Goals, this gives valuable insight into what is working in lead generation.

## Exporting Data for Advanced Analysis

Main:Settings:Export lets you export virtually everything in RealtyJuggler. The export format is normally CSV, which can be viewed in any spreadsheet program such as Microsoft Excel, Mac Numbers or Google Sheets.

To analyze your lead sources in more detail, export the Contacts, Prospects, Buyers, Listings and Closings databases. The Contacts database has a "Referred By" column with details on who referred the lead to you. For each of the other databases, the "Prospect Source" and "Prospect Source Description" columns hold the same information.

## Distributing Leads to Others

You can configure RealtyJuggler as a lead clearinghouse, distributing leads from a central database to assistants and other agents:

1. Create a master RealtyJuggler account.
2. Create as many assistant/agent accounts as needed.
3. In each assistant/agent account, go to Main:Settings:Sharing, add the master account's email address to the sharing field, and click the "Update:" button to lock in the change. Repeat for each assistant/agent account.

When an assistant or agent signs in, they see things just as before. When you sign into the master account, an "Accessing" drop-down menu appears in the upper-right corner. It lists several email addresses: one for your master account and one for each assistant/agent account. Changing the drop-down lets you access each assistant account to supervise it and monitor progress.

When Prospects are added to the master account, you can transfer them to any assistant/agent account. Open the Prospect record, click the "Transfer" tab, and use the "Move To:" drop-down menu to change ownership of the Prospect from you to one of your assistants.

> Image: Screenshot of the transfer section titled "Move This Prospect to Another Account". It has two drop-downs: "Move To:" and "Send a Duplicate To:", both showing "-- Select Destination --".

In this scenario the master account can see all other accounts, and each assistant/agent account can access only its own information. Several variations are possible depending on your needs; this is the most common configuration for lead distribution. More information is in Video Tutorial 09.

## Supervising Others

RealtyJuggler Sharing can be configured so a supervising broker or agent can watch any number of assistant accounts. Configuration starts the same way as in Distributing Leads to Others.

Once sharing is configured, the supervisor can switch between accounts using the "Accessing:" menu in the upper-right corner of their account. The easiest way to get a full view of a deal is to open the individual record and click the "Service Report" tab. It shows every email, every printed letter, and all pending and completed tasks. The supervisor can also see every note entered, giving a comprehensive view of what is happening with the deal.

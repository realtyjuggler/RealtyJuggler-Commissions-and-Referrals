# Chapter 1: Commissions and Other Income

## Commissions and Other Income

> Image: The Income icon, a pie chart with a dark slice and a blue slice and a white percent sign, with the label "Income" beneath it.

RealtyJuggler lets you track your commissions and other income. A summary is available by clicking the Income icon on the Main screen. The Income screen can be filtered in two ways: by selecting from the "Show:" drop-down in the upper-right corner, or by clicking one of the date range tabs along the top. You can sort by any column by clicking its heading.

Commission entries are generated automatically from your Buyer, Listing and Closing records. Other income can be added by clicking the "New Other Income" button at the top of the screen.

Items in red are estimated. For example, Buyer records have an estimated commission based on the price range the buyer is looking at. Once you have picked up your commission check, the commission amount changes from red to black.

Commission calculations take into account any referral fees you pay.

For referrals you have made, you have two options:

- Enter the referral income as Other Income, or
- Enter a Buyer, Listing or Closing record with a status of GaveReferral.

The final result is a comprehensive view of your income and anticipated income.

You can export this information to a CSV file, which can be viewed in Microsoft Excel. This is useful for tax preparation and for detailed analysis of your data.

## Other Income

To add income other than a commission, click the "New Other Income" button from the Commission screen.

> Image: A button labeled "New Other Income" in blue text on a light gray rounded background.

> Image: Screenshot of the Other Income form. Buttons across the top are "Done", "Delete Other Income" and "Duplicate Other Income". Fields are Extra Status, Date (today), Description, Other Income Amount $, "Received Other Income Check:" (No Date), Client First Name, Client Last Name, Property Address and a large Notes box.

This screen lets you enter any miscellaneous income you want to track.

Fill in the "Received Other Income Check" field with the date you were paid. The Income summary screen then changes that income value from Pending to Accrued. More information on tracking income and commissions is in Video Tutorial 26.

## Calculating Commissions

Commission calculations can be complex and vary by brokerage and region. RealtyJuggler has a straightforward calculator that makes it easy to compute both estimated and actual commissions. It is available from the Commission tab in any Buyer, Listing or Closing record.

To compute your commission, fill in the blanks and click the "Calculate Commission" button. The computations are done in the order the fields are displayed. Several drop-down menus let you choose whether individual computations are done as a percentage or a fixed dollar value.

If you fill in a referral fee value, additional fields appear so you can specify the referral direction.

The calculator has several options for the commission rate. In the simplest case, fill in a percentage. For some situations you may need a graduated sales commission. To use one, change the "Sales Commission %" drop-down menu to "Graduated Sales Commission%". Two additional fields appear; fill them in for a two-tiered graduated sales commission.

> Image: Screenshot of the graduated commission fields. The first row is a "Graduated Sales Commission" drop-down with 3.0 and the label "(Other Side)". The second row reads "for the first $" with 150000 and "in sale price". The third row reads "and %" with 2 and "for the remainder".

When you view the Commission tab for a Closing, you see a "Received Check" date field. Fill it in once you receive your commission check. More information is in Video Tutorial 28.

Note: To zero a value out, enter the value 0. If you empty a field, default values are loaded from your preferences.

## Commission Defaults

Most agents have a fixed set of default values for their commission calculations, such as sales commission amount, split and franchise fee amount. To enter your own defaults, go to Main:Settings:Preferences:Commissions.

> Image: Screenshot of the commission defaults form. Rows are Sales Commission % (3.0), Your Split % (100), "Agent & Broker Split Franchise Fee of:" (6, with a % sign) and two "Other Deduction $" rows that are empty.

Fill in the defaults that make sense for your situation. You can override them for any individual deal. They are only the starting point the Commission calculator uses.

## International and Canadian Calculations

Several settings are useful for non-US real estate agents. When you sign up for RealtyJuggler, specify your country accurately, because some fields are available only to non-US customers and others only to Canadian customers. To see which country you selected, click the Account link in the upper-right corner of the screen.

> Image: Screenshot of commission tax fields. They show "Received Commission Check: today", then "Commission Tax % (Net)" set to 6.00 "as a % of Net Commission", then "Total Commission Tax $ 253.80", then "Paid Commission Tax $ No Date".

To track commission taxes, fill in the appropriate value for your deals at the bottom of the Commission screen. An automatic task reminds Canadian agents to pay their commission taxes.

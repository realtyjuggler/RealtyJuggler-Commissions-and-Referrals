# Chapter 2: Income Access and Exporting

## Limiting Assistant Access to Income Information

> Image: The Sharing icon, two dark-blue head-and-shoulders silhouettes inside curved arrows on a light blue square, with the label "Sharing" beneath it.

You can share your information with assistants and partners by going to Main:Settings:Sharing from your master account. Add the email address of any other RealtyJuggler account you want to give access to in the bottom fields, and follow the instructions on that screen.

Before adding an email address for sharing, make sure a RealtyJuggler account has been created with that email address.

To let an assistant see general deal information but not income and expenses, use the drop-down menus to limit access. More information is in Video Tutorial 08.

Eight rows below have an empty email box, an Access drop-down set to "Read+Write+Delete+Export", Income and Expenses drop-downs set to "Show" and an Alarms drop-down set to "No". An "Update" button sits at the bottom with red text beside it reading "<<<<<<< CLICK UPDATE BUTTON TO SAVE".

## Exporting Commissions and Income

> Image: The Export icon, a house outline with a red arrow and a dark arrow pointing down beneath it, with the label "Export" beneath it.

Export your Commissions and Income at Main:Settings:Export. The exported information can be opened in any spreadsheet program, such as Microsoft Excel.

> Image: Screenshot of the Export form with a "Done" button. "Data to Export:" is set to Income, "Format:" is "Comma Separated Values (.csv)", "Year:" is All, and an "Export" button is at the bottom.

This information is useful when preparing your taxes. You can also use it for detailed trend analysis with third-party tools such as Excel. More information is in Video Tutorial 26.
